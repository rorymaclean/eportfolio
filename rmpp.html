Learning outcomes

  1. 1. Appraise the professional, legal, social, cultural and ethical issues that affect computing professionals
  2. 2. Appraise the principles of academic investigation, applying them to a research topic in the applicable computing field
  3. 3. Evaluate critically existing literature, research design and methodology for the chosen topic, including data analysis processes
  4. 4. Produce and evaluate critically the resulting research proposal for the chosen topic
