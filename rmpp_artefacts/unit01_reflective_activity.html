Reflective Activity 1 – Ethics in Computing in the age of Generative AI The generative AI revolution has thrust AI ethics into the fore, due to the impending impact on society. The first response has been to create AI ethics guidelines, which have come from a variety of public and private institutions (Corrêa et al., 2023). Correa and authors, in 2023, explored the landscape of global AI ethics guidelines (Corrêa et al., 2023). They found 200 guidelines, and extracted 17 themes, illustrated in Fig. 1. Key points from their research were that integration of ethical principles will be necessary to present a strong framework on which to develop regulations. This is a global task given the scalability and portability of AI systems. Deckard, in 2023, explored field of AI ethics, and made suggestions to support the future development of the field (Deckard, 2023). Deckard focuses on the career profile of an AI ethicist, highlighting the need for inter-disciplinary skills including ethics, technology and social science. To make progress in such a dynamic field, they suggest that a participatory approach to policy development and good communication will be critical. Based on this reading, it seems that priorities to advance AI ethics should be: 1. Integration of ethical principles such as fairness, transparency, and accountability into regulation. Thereby making these principles legally binding. Regulation is likely to be the more enforceable approach. Note that regulation is often specific to ‘verticals’ e.g. healthcare, but the EU has taken a ‘horizontal’ approach with the cross-cutting EU AI Act (Edwards, 2022). 2. Education in AI ethics. We need experts with cross-modal skills in ethics and AI technology, to research and advance the field. Proactive engagement with innovators and regulatory bodies will support ethical development. 3. Public policy development. There must be a balance between innovation and regulation. Co-development with innovators and AI ethics professions may help to get the balance right. Figure 1.
